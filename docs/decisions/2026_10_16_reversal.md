# The reversal: the agent learns again when the food moves

Date: 2026-10-16. Status: rules written before any training run. The owner chose this step and
this design on 2026-10-16.

## Why

AE-1 of the indicator rubric asks for "learning from feedback and selecting outputs so as to pursue
goals, especially with flexible responsiveness to competing goals". The rubric names two
weaknesses, learning from feedback and flexibility. The value gate PASSED the first in one room: the
agent learned from reward which of two discs that look the same gives food
(`docs/results/value_gate_2026_10.md`). This test asks for flexibility. When the food moves from the
light to the decoy, does the agent change its choice from feedback alone? It is not the "competing
goals" clause (two needs at the same time), which is a later test.

## Sources

| Source | Read | Statement used here |
|---|---|---|
| Kim and Hikosaka, Neuron 79, 1001 (2013) | Full text | Object values reversed every 30 to 40 trials without a cue. Caudate head neurons quickly acquired the new value bias, in parallel with behaviour. Inactivating the caudate head removed only the flexible value bias. The value gate is modelled on this fast learner |
| Matsumoto and Hikosaka, Nature 447, 1111 (2007) | Abstract | When the rewarded and unrewarded positions switched, lateral habenula and dopamine neurons reversed their responses as behaviour reversed. The habenula drives the dopamine dips of negative errors |
| Lopez, Broglio, Rodriguez, Thinus-Blanc and Salas (2000) | Abstract | In goldfish, telencephalic ablation impaired spatial reversal but not cued reversal. A cued reversal needs no pallium |
| Okada et al., Nat. Commun. 5, 3778 (2014) | Abstract | Dorsomedial striatal cholinergic interneurons hold an acquired contingency. Without them, reversal and extinction were faster and acquisition did not change |
| Frank, Seeberger and O'Reilly, Science 306, 1940 (2004) | Abstract | Learning from positive errors (D1, direct pathway) and from negative errors (D2, indirect pathway) can differ |

## Designs compared

| Design | What changes | Reversal here? |
|---|---|---|
| **The unchanged value gate, with a room flag that moves the food (chosen)** | The room only | Yes, by the abstract model below |
| Separate learning rates for positive and negative errors | `models/core/value_gate.py` | Faster unlearning, with a new rate that has no measured value |
| A second, stable value store (caudate tail) | `models/core/value_gate.py` | It resists a reversal; it models habit, another question |
| A cortex-like module that updates the unchosen object | A new module | Not needed for a cued reversal (Lopez 2000), and against the biology-first rule |

The chosen design tests what exists before anything is built. If it fails, separate learning rates
are the next design.

## The room flag (`simulations/environments/simple_visual_env.py`)

`--dark-room-food-switch N`: from episode N on (counted from 0, as the decoy levels are), the decoy
gives the food (1.0 inside its radius) and the light gives -0.01. `info["_truth_food"]` names the
food object. The switch draws nothing, so the room, its pixels and its sound do not change. Needs
`--dark-room-decoy-visible`. The value gate, the binding and the motor map do not change.

## Gate

Seeds 701, 702 and 703, 60 episodes, one run at a time. The flags of the value gate gate: the Gate
B7 flags with `--dark-room-decoy-sound --dark-room-decoy-level 0.5,3.0 --dark-room-decoy-visible
--dark-room-start-between --dark-room-pulse --dark-room-sound-direction off --tectal-orienting value
--tectal-orienting-idle still --map-binding rhythm`, and each arm's flags:

| Arm | Flags | Prediction |
|---|---|---|
| reversal | `--dark-room-food-switch 30` | light first in episodes 21 to 30, decoy first in 46 to 60 |
| stay | none | light first throughout |

First object: within the first 60 steps, the first object closer than 60 room pixels (the nearer
one on a tie); no contact counts as neither. Read by `scripts/analysis/probe_reversal.py`.

| Check | Rule, at 3 of 3 seeds |
|---|---|
| R1 learns before the move | reversal arm, episodes 21 to 30: light first ≥ 0.8 |
| R2 reverses | reversal arm, episodes 46 to 60: decoy first ≥ 0.8 |
| R3 the change follows the food | decoy first in episodes 46 to 60, reversal arm above the stay arm, pooled one-sided Fisher p < 0.05, difference > 0 at each seed |

PASS when R1 to R3 hold. FAIL when one check fails at 2 or more seeds. Reported only: old object
first in episodes 31 to 35, episodes until 4 of 5 reach the food first, the learned values, the
share of steps at the food object, the workspace leader changes. A control with all new flags off
must give the md5 `7825766f42d3d85b032f8028c94dc1e0` for `metrics.csv` of seed 73.

## Limits, stated before the runs

- One reversal, at a fixed episode, in one room. Serial reversals (learning to reverse faster) are
  not tested.
- The light and the decoy differ only by their tone, and the rhythms are steady (binding failed
  with irregular rhythms, `docs/results/rhythm_binding_harder_rooms_2026_10.md`).
- The orienting stays a reflex; the value gate decides which target it gets. The policy's movement
  network is not used (`docs/results/action_core_relay_defect_2026_10.md`).
- A passed gate does not move AE-1 by itself. The score is the owner's decision.

## Note added after the runs (2026-10-17)

The flag counted every reset, as the decoy levels do. `scripts/training/train_rlhf.py` resets the
room once before its first episode (`seed_environment`), so in the gate runs
`--dark-room-food-switch 30` moved the food at episode index 29. The offline diagnosis made no such
reset. The rules above are unchanged. `docs/results/reversal_2026_10.md` reports the gate as
written, and again with every window moved by one episode. The flag now counts only the episodes in
which the room runs a step.
